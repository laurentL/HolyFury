# Holy Fury

**Never tank without Righteous Fury again.**

Holy Fury is a small, focused add-on for paladins on **WoW Forever, Classic Era (Season of Discovery, Hardcore), Burning Crusade Anniversary and Mists of Pandaria Classic**. When Righteous Fury drops while you tank, you can't miss it: a big flashing icon, a raid warning message and a sound. During its last minute, a countdown tells you it is about to run out.

## Features

- **Impossible to miss:** large icon with a red frame and "RIGHTEOUS FURY!" in the middle of your screen, raid warning message and sound. The sound repeats every 20 seconds until you recast it.
- **Countdown:** the icon turns gold during the last minute and shows the seconds left (not in Mists, where Righteous Fury lasts until cancelled).
- **Dual spec aware:** it only watches in your tank spec. By default, that is the spec where Protection has the most talent points (or the Protection specialization in Mists). In a Holy or Retribution spec, it stays quiet. You can also pick your tank spec by hand with `/hf spec`.
- **Only where it matters:** in dungeons and raids by default, or everywhere if you prefer.
- **Reliable in combat:** if the game hides aura details during a fight, Holy Fury uses the expiry time it read before the pull.
- **Movable:** put the alert wherever you like.
- **10 languages, picked automatically:** English, French, German, Spanish (EU and Latin America), Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
- **Lightweight:** no libraries, no dependencies, settings saved per character.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/hf` | Show the current settings, your talent points and the help |
| `/hf test` | Show the alert to preview it (type it again to hide it) |
| `/hf move` | Unlock the alert and drag it; type it again to lock |
| `/hf dungeon` / `always` / `off` | Watch in dungeons and raids, everywhere, or never |
| `/hf sound` | Turn the sound off or on |
| `/hf spec` | Make your current spec the tank spec (`/hf spec auto` to go back to automatic detection) |

`/holyfury` works too. Command words also work in your language, for example `/hf donjon` or `/hf verschieben`.

## Supported versions

WoW Forever, Classic Era (Season of Discovery, Hardcore), Burning Crusade Anniversary, Wrath (Titan) and Mists of Pandaria Classic. Not for Retail: Righteous Fury no longer exists there.

## Français

**Ne tanke plus jamais sans Fureur vertueuse.**

Holy Fury est un petit addon pour les paladins de **WoW Forever, Classic Era (Saison de la Découverte, Hardcore), Burning Crusade Anniversary et Mists of Pandaria Classic**. Quand la Fureur vertueuse disparaît pendant que tu tankes, tu ne peux pas la rater : grosse icône qui clignote, message d'avertissement de raid et son. Pendant sa dernière minute, un compte à rebours t'indique qu'elle va expirer.

- **Spé tank uniquement :** l'addon surveille quand Protection est ton arbre le plus rempli. Tu peux aussi choisir ta spé tank avec `/hf spe`.
- **En donjon et raid** par défaut, ou partout avec `/hf partout`.
- **Commandes en français :** `/hf test`, `/hf deplacer`, `/hf donjon`, `/hf partout`, `/hf off`, `/hf son`, `/hf spe`.
- **Langue automatique :** l'addon s'affiche dans la langue de ton client.
